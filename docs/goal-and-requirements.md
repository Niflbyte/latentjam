## Goal

Give people an offline player for music they already own, with sound-aware sequencing and rediscovery rather than relying on streaming services or accounts. This is inferred from the [project README](../README.md) and implementation; the imported specification is unconfirmed.

## Capabilities

- Play and browse a local library on Android and iOS, with persistent queue and playlists.
- Analyze audio and metadata on device for SMART sequencing, search, mixes and a library map.
- Edit supported file tags, read embedded or sidecar lyrics, find duplicates, and export or restore local data.
- Provide listening history, statistics and rediscovery without uploading listening data.

## Principles and scope

The app is free and offline, with no account, advertising or analytics. Platform capabilities differ, particularly for iOS Music-library items. Streaming and online music discovery are outside the described product scope; this characterization is inferred, not separately confirmed.
